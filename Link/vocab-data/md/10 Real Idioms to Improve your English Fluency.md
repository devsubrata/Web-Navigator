# 10 Real Idioms to Improve your English Fluency

<h2 style="text-align: center; padding: 5px; border-top: 3px solid tomato; border-bottom: 3px solid tomato; color: blue; margin: 50px;">10 Real Idioms</h2>

1️⃣**to grab a bite (to eat)** ➜ to get something to eat quickly  
 ⟹ Let's grab a bite before we go to the cinema.  
 ⟹ **Part 1 (Food)** — If I get up late, I'll grab a bite at a coffee shop on the way to work.  
 ⟹ **Part 2 (Describe a time you were in a hurry)** — I grabbed a bite at a small Italian restaurant.  
 ⟹ **Part 3 (Eating habits)** — Nowadays, people often grab a bite instead of cooking at home.  
<br>  
2️⃣**to be broke** ➜ to have no money  
 ⟹ I can't go out to the pub, I'm broke.  
 ⟹ **Part 1 (Money)** — When I was a student, I was often broke.  
 ⟹ **Part 2 (Describe a difficult time)** — After losing my job, I was totally broke.  
 ⟹ **Part 3 (Finance and youth)** — Nowadays, many young people are broke because of high living costs.  
<br>  
3️⃣**to pick holes (in something)** ➜ to find small mistakes or weakness in something (often unfairly or critically)  
 ⟹ He loves picking holes in everything I say.  
 ⟹ **Part 1 (Personality)** — I try not to pick holes in other people's ideas.  
 ⟹ **Part 2 (Describe a disagreement)** — During the meeting, my boss picked holes in my proposal.  
 ⟹ **Part 3 (Critical thinking / team work)** — In meetings, some people focus picking holes instead of offering solutions.  
<br>  
4️⃣**to be under the weather** ➜ to feel slightly ill  
 ⟹ I'm a bit under the weather today.  
 ⟹ **Part 1 (Health)** — If I'm under the weather, I usually stay at home and rest.  
 ⟹ **Part 2 (Describe a time you were sick)** — Last week I felt under the weather for a few days, during my holiday.  
 ⟹ **Part 3 (Health and lifestyle)** — People often feel under the weather when they don't get enough sleep.  
<br>  
5️⃣**to be swamped (with something)** ➜ to be extremely busy  
 ⟹ I am going to be swamped with emails / meetings / reports / work after this holiday again.  
 ⟹ **Part 1 (Work or Study)** — I've been swamped with assignments lately.  
 ⟹ **Part 2 (Describe a busy time)** — When I started my new job, I was swamped with tasks.  
 ⟹ **Part 3 (Modern life)** — Nowadays, we are swamped with information because of the internet.  
<br>  
6️⃣**to make up your mind** ➜ to decide  
 ⟹ She can't make up her mind. / I can't make up my mind what to say.  
 ⟹ **Part 1 (Decisions)** — I am terrible at making up my mind.  
 ⟹ **Part 2 (Describe a difficult decision)** — I had to make up my mind about challenging jobs.  
 ⟹ **Part 3 (Decision-making)** — People need to take time to make up their minds about major life choices.  
<br>  
7️⃣**to be in hot water / to get into hot water** ➜ to be in trouble or have problems (often punished)  
 ⟹ I got into hot water for missing the deadline.  
 ⟹ **Part 1 (Work / School)** — I was in hot water for being late.  
 ⟹ **Part 2 (Describe a difficult situation)** — I got into hot water when I forgot to submit my assignment.  
 ⟹ **Part 3 (Society)** — Politicians often get into hot water for saying the wrong thing.  
<br>  
8️⃣**to be up in the air** ➜ uncertain; not yet decided (plans, choices, etc...)  
 ⟹ Our travel plans are still up in the air.  
 ⟹ **Part 1 (Plans)** — My weekend plans are up in the air at the moment.  
 ⟹ **Part 2 (Describe future plans)** — I am thinking about moving abroad, but it is still up in the air.  
 ⟹ **Part 3 (Decision-making / society)** — Many people's career plans are up in the air because of the economy.  
<br>  
9️⃣**to keep your chin up** ➜ stay positive in a difficult situation  
 ⟹ Don't worry, things will get better, keep your chin up!  
 ⟹ **Part 1 (Difficulties)** — Even when I fail, I try to keep my chin up.  
 ⟹ **Part 2 (Describe a challenge)** — When I lost my job, my family encouraged me to keep my chin up.  
 ⟹ **Part 3 (Resilience)** — It's important for people to keep their chin up in tough times. <br>  
🔟**to touch base (with someone)** ➜ to make brief contact with someone to update or check on something  
 ⟹ Let's touch base next week and finalize our plan.  
 ⟹ **Part 1 (Friends / Communication)** — I live away from home but I try and touch base with my family at least once a week.  
 ⟹ **Part 2 (Describe a work or study project)** — I had to touch base with my manager to confirm the details.  
 ⟹ **Part 3 (Workplace / Collaboration)** — In business, it's important to touch base regularly so everyone stays on track.
